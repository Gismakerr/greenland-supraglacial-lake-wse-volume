# Data sources and redistribution

## Required external source products (not redistributed)

1. Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface-reflectance imagery: Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem / ESA.
2. SWOT Level-2 HR PIXC and Level-1B HR SLC products: NASA/JPL PO.DAAC.
3. ICESat-2 ATL03/ATL06 products: NASA NSIDC DAAC.
4. ArcticDEM or the DEM product documented in the workflow: Polar Geospatial Center and its stated license.

The release contains only compact, derived observations, fitted models, summaries, and vector products required to interpret and reproduce the paper-level results. Product identifiers and acquisition filters should be recovered from the workflow records and official provider metadata when rebuilding from source.
